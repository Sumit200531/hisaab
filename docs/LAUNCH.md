# Getting real users

Hisaab is free and needs no sign-up, so the only barrier is a shopkeeper trying it. This is a plan for the first 50 shops, and what to measure so you can talk about real usage in interviews.

## Week 1: five shops you can walk to

1. **Pick the right shops.** Kirana stores, milk and dairy sellers, tiffin services, a dhobi or a tuition teacher: anyone with 10+ regular customers who pay later. Your own family's regular shop is the best first user.
2. **Install it for them.** Open the link on their phone, tap *Add to home screen*, and type their shop name and UPI ID. Add three of their real udhaar customers with them, in their language.
3. **Show the one thing that sells it.** Tap *WhatsApp पर याद दिलाएँ* on a customer who owes money. The shopkeeper sees a polite reminder with a pay link that opens Google Pay or PhonePe for the exact amount. That moment is the product.
4. **Set up safety.** Tap *बैकअप भेजें* and send the backup to their own WhatsApp. Tell them: "phone kho gaya to bhi hisaab rahega."

## Weeks 2–4: learn, fix, then grow

- **Visit again after 3 days.** Ask: "Kal kitni entry ki? Kya mushkil laga?" Watch them add one entry without helping. Every hesitation is a bug to fix.
- **Measure only what matters,** and count it by asking, not tracking. Hisaab has no analytics, on purpose:
  - shops that still use it after 7 days;
  - entries per shop per day;
  - reminders sent;
  - shopkeepers who say they collected faster.
- **Grow through the shops.** Ask each happy shopkeeper for one more shop in their market. Shopkeepers trust other shopkeepers.
- **Make a 30-second video.** Film a real shopkeeper (with permission) adding udhaar by voice and sending a reminder. Post it on WhatsApp status, Instagram reels and local Facebook groups.

## How to talk about it

Use real numbers only, for example: *"Used daily by 23 shops in my area; one dairy recovered ₹14,000 of old dues in the first month using reminders."* An interviewer will ask how you know. Your answer is that you visited the shops and counted with them. That is worth more than any download number.

## Privacy promise to repeat to every shop

"Aapka hisaab sirf aapke phone mein hai. Sync chalu karo to bhi woh taale mein (encrypted) jaata hai — hum bhi nahi padh sakte."
